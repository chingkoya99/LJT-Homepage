I am **Junteng Liu**, a first-year PhD candidate in the [HKUST NLP Group](https://hkust-nlp.github.io/) at the [Hong Kong University of Science and Technology](https://hkust.edu.hk/) (HKUST), advised by [Professor Junxian He](https://jxhe.github.io/). I received my B.Eng. from [Shanghai Jiao Tong University](https://www.sjtu.edu.cn/) (SJTU) in June 2024, where I was also advised by Professor Junxian He.

My research lies at the intersection of **natural language processing** and **machine learning**. I am broadly interested in understanding and improving large language models.
